Outreach works when three things go right: you find the right contact, your email reaches the inbox, and your message is worth replying to. These tools cover the first two and help you scale the third.

## Finding contacts

### Hunter

![Hunter](/images/tools/hunter.png)

Enter a company domain and Hunter shows the email addresses it has found, the most likely pattern (like first.last@), and a confidence score. Its verifier checks addresses before you send.

### Snov.io

![Snov.io](/images/tools/snov-io.png)

Combines an email finder, a verifier, a Chrome extension and drip campaigns, so you can go from prospect list to sent sequence in one place.

### Wiza

![Wiza](/images/tools/wiza.png)

Turns LinkedIn searches into spreadsheets of contacts with verified emails.

## Sending outreach

### Saleshandy

![Saleshandy](/images/tools/saleshandy.png)

Cold email sequences with inbox rotation across multiple sending accounts, which spreads volume and protects deliverability.

### Mailrush

![Mailrush](/images/tools/mailrush.png)

A cold email platform that includes help with deliverability setup and warm-up.

### Pitchbox and Postaga

![Pitchbox](/images/tools/pitchbox.png)

Both are built for outreach campaigns like link building, PR and partnerships, with prospecting and follow-up tracking built in.

## Capturing inbound contacts

### Jotform

![Jotform](/images/tools/jotform.png)

Your own website is an outreach channel too. Jotform makes contact, quote and booking forms in minutes and sends submissions wherever you need them.

### Hello Bar

![Hello Bar](/images/tools/hello-bar.png)

Adds a bar or pop-up to your site to collect emails or promote an offer.

## Outreach rules that keep you out of spam

- Send from a separate domain or subdomain and authenticate it with SPF, DKIM and DMARC.
- Verify every address before you send.
- Keep daily volume per inbox low and increase it gradually.
- Personalize the first line. A generic template gets ignored.
- Include a clear way to opt out, and follow the anti-spam laws where your recipients live.
