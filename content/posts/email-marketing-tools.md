Social platforms can change their algorithm overnight. Your email list is yours. The right tool depends on how complex your automations need to be and how much you want to spend as your list grows.

## MailerLite

![MailerLite](/images/tools/mailerlite.png)

A clean editor, landing pages, forms and automations, with a free plan for small lists. It's one of the best-value options for small businesses.

**Best for:** getting started without a big bill.

## Kit (formerly ConvertKit)

![Kit](/images/tools/kit.png)

Built for creators. Tag-based subscriber management, visual automations, and tools for selling digital products and paid newsletters.

**Best for:** bloggers, YouTubers and course creators.

## ActiveCampaign

![ActiveCampaign](/images/tools/activecampaign.png)

The most powerful automation builder here, with conditional logic, lead scoring and a built-in CRM.

**Best for:** businesses with complex customer journeys.

## AWeber

![AWeber](/images/tools/aweber.png)

One of the original email services, with reliable deliverability, lots of templates and good support.

**Best for:** small businesses that want a dependable, simple tool.

## Sender

![Sender](/images/tools/sender.png)

Email and SMS in one place, with a free plan and good e-commerce integrations.

**Best for:** online stores on a budget.

## Elastic Email

![Elastic Email](/images/tools/elastic-email.png)

Very low-cost sending for both marketing campaigns and transactional emails like receipts and password resets.

**Best for:** high volume at low cost.

## Stripo

![Stripo](/images/tools/stripo.png)

Not a sender but a designer: build responsive email templates and export them to most of the platforms above.

**Best for:** teams that want better-looking emails.

## Emails every list should have

1. **Welcome sequence:** 3 to 5 emails that introduce you and deliver your best content.
2. **Regular newsletter:** a consistent schedule your subscribers can expect.
3. **Sales sequence:** for each offer, a short series that explains the problem, the solution and the deadline.
4. **Re-engagement:** a check-in for subscribers who haven't opened in months, then clean the list.
