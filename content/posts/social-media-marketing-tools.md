Social media marketing is a cycle: find out what your audience cares about, create content for it, publish consistently, and learn from the results. These tools help at each step.

## Research: BuzzSumo

![BuzzSumo](/images/tools/buzzsumo.png)

Search any topic and BuzzSumo shows the most-shared articles and posts, who shared them, and which formats performed best. It's a fast way to find proven ideas before you create anything.

## Scheduling: ContentStudio

![ContentStudio](/images/tools/contentstudio.png)

Plan and schedule posts across the major networks from one calendar. It also suggests trending content in your niche to share, and supports team approval workflows.

## Design: Picsart

![Picsart](/images/tools/picsart.png)

Quick graphics, background removal and AI image tools for posts, stories and thumbnails, on desktop or mobile.

## Lead capture: Hello Bar

![Hello Bar](/images/tools/hello-bar.png)

Social traffic is only valuable if you keep it. A Hello Bar on your landing page turns visitors from social into email subscribers.

## Research and listening: Speak AI

![Speak AI](/images/tools/speak-ai.png)

Transcribe your videos, lives and podcasts, then reuse the transcripts as captions, quotes and blog posts.

## A word on buying followers and views

Services like Media Mister, GetAFollower, SubPals and Stormviews sell followers, subscribers, likes or views. We list them only to be clear: purchased engagement breaks most platforms' terms, does not buy real customers, and can hurt reach because the algorithm sees an audience that doesn't engage. Put that budget into content or ads instead.

## A simple weekly routine

1. **Monday:** check what performed last week and what's trending in BuzzSumo.
2. **Tuesday:** batch-create the week's graphics and captions.
3. **Wednesday:** schedule everything in one calendar.
4. **Daily:** spend 15 minutes replying to comments and messages.
