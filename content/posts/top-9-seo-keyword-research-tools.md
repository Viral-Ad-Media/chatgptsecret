Keyword research tells you what people actually type into search, how hard each phrase is to rank for, and which topics your competitors already own. Pick the wrong keyword and even a great article can sit on page five forever.

All of the tools below give you search volume and some form of difficulty score. They differ in data sources, ease of use and how much else they bundle in.

## 1. KWFinder

![KWFinder](/images/tools/kwfinder.png)

Mangools' keyword tool is the easiest to learn. Type a seed keyword and you get related ideas, volume trends and a clear difficulty score backed by a look at the current top 10.

**Best for:** beginners and bloggers.

## 2. SE Ranking

![SE Ranking](/images/tools/se-ranking.png)

Its keyword research module includes similar, related and low-volume suggestions, plus a view of what competitors rank for.

**Best for:** people who also want rank tracking and audits.

## 3. Serpstat

![Serpstat](/images/tools/serpstat.png)

A broad platform covering organic and paid keywords, with competitor gap analysis and keyword clustering.

**Best for:** marketers who run both SEO and PPC.

## 4. Long Tail Pro

![Long Tail Pro](/images/tools/long-tail-pro.png)

Built around finding longer, lower-competition phrases that newer sites can realistically win.

**Best for:** niche sites and new domains.

## 5. Jaaxy

![Jaaxy](/images/tools/jaaxy.png)

Popular with affiliate marketers. It reports average searches and a "QSR" count of competing pages, plus domain availability checks.

**Best for:** affiliate and niche-site builders.

## 6. RankIQ

![RankIQ](/images/tools/rankiq.png)

Gives bloggers a curated library of low-competition, high-traffic keywords by niche, then grades your draft against the top results.

**Best for:** food, lifestyle and hobby bloggers.

## 7. Wordtracker

![Wordtracker](/images/tools/wordtracker.png)

Pulls keyword data from Google, Amazon and YouTube, which helps when you sell products or make video.

**Best for:** e-commerce and YouTube creators.

## 8. Twinword Ideas

![Twinword Ideas](/images/tools/twinword-ideas.png)

Groups keyword ideas by topic and labels user intent, which makes it easier to plan content clusters.

**Best for:** content planners building topic clusters.

## 9. Morningscore

![Morningscore](/images/tools/morningscore.png)

Shows the keywords you already rank for and the ones closest to page one, so you can prioritize quick wins.

**Best for:** small businesses improving an existing site.

## A simple keyword research workflow

1. **Start with problems, not keywords.** List the questions your customers ask.
2. **Expand** each question in a tool to find variations and long-tail phrases.
3. **Filter** by difficulty your site can realistically beat.
4. **Check intent** by looking at the current top results. If they're all product pages, a blog post won't rank.
5. **Group** similar keywords into one page instead of writing near-duplicates.
