A CRM (customer relationship management system) is where every lead, customer and conversation lives. Add automation and it can follow up for you, move deals forward and alert you when something needs attention.

## HighLevel

![HighLevel](/images/tools/highlevel.png)

An all-in-one platform for agencies: CRM, pipelines, funnels, email, SMS, booking and reputation management, which you can white-label and resell to clients.

**Best for:** marketing agencies.

## ActiveCampaign

![ActiveCampaign](/images/tools/activecampaign.png)

Combines one of the best email automation builders with a sales CRM, lead scoring and deal pipelines.

**Best for:** businesses whose sales start with email marketing.

## Streak

![Streak](/images/tools/streak.png)

A CRM that runs inside Gmail. Emails become pipeline items, and you never leave your inbox.

**Best for:** small teams that live in Gmail.

## Systeme.io

![Systeme.io](/images/tools/systeme-io.png)

Funnels, email, courses and a simple contact database in one inexpensive package, with a free plan.

**Best for:** solo entrepreneurs who want simplicity.

## Make and Zapier

![Make](/images/tools/make.png)

These connect your CRM to everything else. Zapier is easier to learn. Make (formerly Integromat) offers more control over complex, branching workflows and is often cheaper at volume.

**Best for:** filling the gaps between your tools.

## Automations worth setting up first

1. **Speed to lead:** text or email new leads within minutes.
2. **Missed-call text-back** for service businesses.
3. **Pipeline reminders** when a deal hasn't moved in a week.
4. **Review requests** after a job or purchase is complete.
5. **Win-back** messages to past customers.
