For B2B sales, LinkedIn is often where your buyers are. Outreach platforms automate the repetitive parts like profile visits, connection requests and follow-up messages, so you can spend your time on the conversations that reply.

## Octopus CRM

![Octopus CRM](/images/tools/octopus-crm.png)

A Chrome-extension tool for LinkedIn that automates connection requests, messages, endorsements and profile visits, with simple funnel reporting.

**Best for:** individuals getting started with LinkedIn outreach.

## Dux-Soup

![Dux-Soup](/images/tools/dux-soup.png)

One of the longest-running LinkedIn automation tools, with drip campaigns and CRM integrations on its higher plans.

**Best for:** sales reps who want CRM sync.

## Zopto

![Zopto](/images/tools/zopto.png)

Cloud-based LinkedIn outreach that runs without your browser open, with advanced filtering and agency features.

**Best for:** agencies running campaigns for clients.

## Salesflow

![Salesflow](/images/tools/salesflow.png)

Cloud campaigns that combine LinkedIn and email, with a focus on safety limits.

**Best for:** teams running outreach for several reps.

## Alfred

![Alfred](/images/tools/alfred.png)

Multi-channel sequences across LinkedIn, email and X, with a shared inbox for replies.

**Best for:** outreach that spans more than one channel.

## Wiza and Snov.io

![Wiza](/images/tools/wiza.png)

When a prospect doesn't accept your connection request, email is the backup. Wiza and Snov.io find and verify the email addresses that go with LinkedIn profiles.

## Make and Zapier

![Zapier](/images/tools/zapier.png)

Connect your outreach tool to your CRM so every reply becomes a lead, and every booked call becomes a deal.

## Staying safe on LinkedIn

LinkedIn's user agreement restricts automated activity, and accounts that send too much too fast get restricted. If you use automation:

- Keep connection requests well under LinkedIn's weekly limits.
- Warm up new accounts gradually.
- Personalize messages. Low acceptance rates are a red flag.
- Prefer cloud tools with dedicated IPs over shared ones.
- Never automate an account you can't afford to lose.
