If you publish content to win search traffic, you need to know where you stand. A rank tracker checks your positions for a list of keywords on a schedule, so you can see whether a page is climbing, stalling or slipping, and catch problems before they show up in your traffic.

Before you choose one, settle three questions:

- **How many keywords and sites?** Most pricing scales with tracked keywords.
- **How often do you need updates?** Daily is standard. Some tools offer on-demand checks.
- **Who reads the reports?** Agencies usually want white-label PDFs and client logins.

## 1. SE Ranking

![SE Ranking](/images/tools/se-ranking.png)

A full SEO suite where rank tracking is the strongest piece. You get daily updates, competitor tracking, SERP feature tracking and tidy reports, plus audits and keyword research in the same account.

**Best for:** small teams that want one tool instead of five.

## 2. Advanced Web Ranking

![Advanced Web Ranking](/images/tools/advanced-web-ranking.png)

One of the most detailed trackers available. It handles many locations, devices and search engines and keeps a long ranking history.

**Best for:** agencies and in-house teams with local or international SEO.

## 3. ProRankTracker

![ProRankTracker](/images/tools/proranktracker.png)

A dedicated tracker with daily checks, mobile and local tracking and white-label reports you can schedule for clients.

**Best for:** freelancers who send monthly reports.

## 4. Rank Tracker by SEO PowerSuite

![Rank Tracker](/images/tools/rank-tracker.png)

A desktop app rather than a web service. Because it runs on your computer, paid plans don't cap the number of keywords.

**Best for:** people tracking large keyword sets on a budget.

## 5. SERPWatcher

![SERPWatcher](/images/tools/serpwatcher.png)

Part of the Mangools suite. It's simple, with a single "dominance index" that shows your overall visibility at a glance.

**Best for:** bloggers who want clarity over complexity.

## 6. Nightwatch

![Nightwatch](/images/tools/nightwatch.png)

Strong segmentation lets you group keywords by page, intent or location and track each segment's visibility over time.

**Best for:** sites with many content clusters.

## 7. Rank Ranger

![Rank Ranger](/images/tools/rank-ranger.png)

Rank Ranger, now part of Similarweb, is known for deep SERP feature data and highly customizable reports.

**Best for:** enterprise teams that need custom reporting.

## 8. Morningscore

![Morningscore](/images/tools/morningscore.png)

Turns SEO into "missions" with a health score. Tracking is easy to read, which makes it a good fit for business owners doing their own SEO.

**Best for:** beginners who want guidance, not just data.

## 9. SEOprofiler

![SEOprofiler](/images/tools/seoprofiler.png)

Ranking monitoring sits alongside audits, backlink checks and competitive research.

**Best for:** small businesses that want an all-rounder.

## 10. Shine Ranker

![Shine Ranker](/images/tools/shine-ranker.png)

A low-cost toolkit with rank tracking, audits and keyword research. It has fewer bells and whistles but covers the basics.

**Best for:** tight budgets.

## 11. Squirrly SEO

![Squirrly SEO](/images/tools/squirrly.png)

A WordPress plugin that shows rankings for your focus keywords inside your dashboard, next to its on-page guidance.

**Best for:** WordPress sites that want everything in one place.

## How to get value from any tracker

1. Track the keywords tied to pages that make money, not vanity terms.
2. Tag keywords by page or topic so you can see which clusters are improving.
3. Look at trends over 4 to 8 weeks. Daily swings are noise.
4. Pair rankings with Google Search Console clicks to see what actually drives traffic.
