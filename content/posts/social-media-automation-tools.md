Automation works best on the parts of social media that are repetitive and predictable: answering the same questions, delivering a freebie when someone comments a keyword, or pulling data out of a platform. It works worst when it pretends to be a human having a real conversation.

## Chat and DM automation

### Manychat

![Manychat](/images/tools/manychat.png)

The best-known tool for Instagram, Facebook Messenger, WhatsApp and SMS automation. A common use is "comment GUIDE and we'll DM it to you", which delivers the link instantly and grows your contact list.

### Botnation

![Botnation](/images/tools/botnation.png)

A no-code chatbot builder for websites and Messenger, with templates for lead qualification and FAQs.

## Data and workflow automation

### PhantomBuster

![PhantomBuster](/images/tools/phantombuster.png)

Ready-made automations ("Phantoms") that extract data from social platforms and run actions on a schedule. Check each platform's terms before you automate actions on it.

### Zapier

![Zapier](/images/tools/zapier.png)

Connects your social tools to everything else: post new blog articles automatically, add new leads to your CRM, or alert your team in Slack.

## Growth services

### Kicksta, Nitreo and FlockSocial

![Kicksta](/images/tools/kicksta.png)

These are managed Instagram growth services that engage with accounts similar to your target audience to attract followers. Results vary. Choose services that work within Instagram's rules, avoid any that ask for your password without clear security practices, and judge them by real engagement rather than follower count.

## Rules for automating well

- Automate replies to predictable triggers, not real conversations.
- Always give people an easy way to reach a human.
- Stay within each platform's limits and terms.
- Review your automations monthly. Outdated messages hurt trust.
