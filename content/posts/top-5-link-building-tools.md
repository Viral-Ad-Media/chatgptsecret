Links from other websites remain one of the strongest ranking signals. Google is also very good at spotting links that were bought or manufactured at scale. The tools below focus on earning links through outreach, relationships and good internal structure.

## 1. Pitchbox

![Pitchbox](/images/tools/pitchbox.png)

An outreach CRM built for link building. It finds prospects, pulls contact details, sends personalized sequences and tracks every conversation.

**Best for:** agencies and teams running outreach at volume.

## 2. Link Whisper

![Link Whisper](/images/tools/link-whisper.png)

Internal links are the links you fully control. This WordPress plugin suggests relevant internal links while you write and flags orphaned pages.

**Best for:** content-heavy WordPress sites.

## 3. FATJOE

![FATJOE](/images/tools/fatjoe.png)

A service rather than software: FATJOE handles blogger outreach, guest posts and digital PR for you. Review the sites they place links on to make sure they're relevant to your niche.

**Best for:** businesses that want to outsource outreach.

## 4. Postaga

![Postaga](/images/tools/postaga.png)

Postaga suggests outreach opportunities based on your content, such as broken links, unlinked mentions and resource pages, and helps you write the pitch.

**Best for:** small teams new to outreach.

## 5. Crowdo

![Crowdo](/images/tools/crowdo.png)

Builds brand mentions and links in relevant forums and communities, which can diversify a link profile and send referral traffic.

**Best for:** adding natural, varied mentions.

## Tools to be careful with

You'll also find automated link software such as GSA Search Engine Ranker, XRumer, Money Robot, SEnuke and Web 2.0 ranker services. They create large numbers of low-quality links automatically. That is exactly the pattern Google's spam policies target, and it can lead to manual penalties or rankings that disappear overnight. We don't recommend them for any site you care about.

## A sustainable link building plan

1. **Create something worth linking to:** original data, a free tool, or the best guide on a topic.
2. **Fix your internal links first.**
3. **Reclaim** unlinked brand mentions and broken links to your site.
4. **Pitch** relevant sites with a clear reason their readers will benefit.
5. **Track** every outreach conversation so follow-ups don't slip.
