A site audit crawls your pages the way a search engine does and flags anything that gets in the way: broken links, redirect chains, duplicate titles, missing meta descriptions, thin pages, slow load times and indexing problems.

Run an audit when you launch, after any redesign or migration, and on a regular schedule after that.

## 1. Screaming Frog SEO Spider

![Screaming Frog](/images/tools/screaming-frog.png)

The industry-standard desktop crawler. It is free for up to 500 URLs, and a paid licence removes the limit and adds JavaScript rendering, custom extraction and integrations with Google Analytics and Search Console.

**Best for:** anyone serious about technical SEO.

## 2. SE Ranking Website Audit

![SE Ranking](/images/tools/se-ranking.png)

A cloud audit that groups issues by severity and explains how to fix each one. Scheduled re-crawls show whether your fixes worked.

**Best for:** teams that want clear, prioritized to-do lists.

## 3. Netpeak Spider

![Netpeak Spider](/images/tools/netpeak-spider.png)

A fast desktop crawler with dozens of built-in checks and good handling of large sites.

**Best for:** Windows users auditing big sites.

## 4. SEOprofiler

![SEOprofiler](/images/tools/seoprofiler.png)

Its audit sits inside a full SEO suite, so you can move straight from a technical issue to the rankings and links it affects.

**Best for:** small businesses that want everything in one login.

## 5. Page Optimizer Pro

![Page Optimizer Pro](/images/tools/page-optimizer-pro.png)

Rather than crawling your whole site, it audits a single page against the pages that rank for your keyword and tells you which terms and elements to add or remove.

**Best for:** pushing important pages from page two to page one.

## 6. Rank Math

![Rank Math](/images/tools/rank-math.png)

The WordPress plugin includes an SEO analyzer that checks your site's basics, and it fixes many common issues for you, from sitemaps to schema.

**Best for:** WordPress sites.

## 7. Squirrly SEO

![Squirrly SEO](/images/tools/squirrly.png)

Squirrly's audit scores your site across areas like blogging, traffic, social and links, and gives you weekly progress reports.

**Best for:** WordPress owners who like guided checklists.

## 8. Morningscore

![Morningscore](/images/tools/morningscore.png)

The health check finds technical errors and ranks them by how much they matter, in plain language.

**Best for:** non-technical business owners.

## What to fix first

1. **Indexing blockers:** pages wrongly set to noindex, blocked in robots.txt, or missing from the sitemap.
2. **Broken pages and links:** 404s, redirect loops and chains.
3. **Speed and Core Web Vitals:** oversized images and render-blocking scripts.
4. **Duplicate or missing titles and descriptions.**
5. **Thin or duplicate content** that should be merged or improved.
