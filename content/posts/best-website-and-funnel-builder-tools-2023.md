A website tells people who you are. A funnel walks them step by step towards buying. Depending on your business you may need one, the other, or both.

## Funnel builders

### ClickFunnels

![ClickFunnels](/images/tools/clickfunnels.png)

The tool that made sales funnels mainstream. Landing pages, order forms, upsells, email, courses and memberships in one place.

**Best for:** info-product and coaching businesses.

### Systeme.io

![Systeme.io](/images/tools/systeme-io.png)

Many of the same features, including funnels, email, courses and affiliate programs, at a much lower price, with a free plan.

**Best for:** beginners and budget-conscious creators.

### HighLevel

![HighLevel](/images/tools/highlevel.png)

Funnels plus a full CRM, SMS and booking, with white-labeling for agencies.

**Best for:** agencies building for clients.

### Convertri and Instapage

![Instapage](/images/tools/instapage.png)

Focused landing page builders. Convertri is built for speed. Instapage is strong on A/B testing and matching pages to ads.

**Best for:** paid traffic campaigns.

### ThriveCart

![ThriveCart](/images/tools/thrivecart.png)

A checkout platform with bumps, upsells, subscriptions and an affiliate center that plugs into your existing site.

**Best for:** selling without changing your website.

## Website builders

### Squarespace

![Squarespace](/images/tools/squarespace.png)

Beautiful templates and built-in commerce, scheduling and email.

### Duda

![Duda](/images/tools/duda.png)

Built for agencies that manage many client sites, with team permissions and client billing.

### Strikingly, Webnode and Ucraft

![Strikingly](/images/tools/strikingly.png)

Simple builders that get a small site or landing page live in an afternoon.

### WordPress with Elementor or Divi

![Elementor](/images/tools/elementor.png)

The most flexible option. WordPress gives you full ownership, and visual builders like Elementor and Divi make design drag-and-drop. You'll also need hosting.

## Hosting for WordPress

![SiteGround](/images/tools/siteground.png)

SiteGround and WP Engine offer managed WordPress with backups, caching and security built in. Hawk Host, HostMetro and LightningBase are smaller, budget-friendly options.

**Whatever you choose, keep regular off-site backups and keep your plugins updated.** An outdated WordPress install is one of the most common ways sites get hacked.

## How to choose

- **Selling one offer?** Start with a funnel builder.
- **Local business or portfolio?** A website builder is enough.
- **Content and SEO-led business?** WordPress or a modern framework gives you the most control.
